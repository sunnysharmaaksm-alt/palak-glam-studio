A Few Helpful Details

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you enquire about your appointment.

How can I book an appointment?

Fill in the booking enquiry form on this website. It will open WhatsApp with your details so Palak can discuss availability and the next steps.

Do you provide home makeup service?

Home service can be discussed for Lucknow locations. Share your venue or area while enquiring so travel and availability can be confirmed.

How early should I book bridal makeup?

Early enquiry is recommended, especially for wedding dates and peak wedding seasons. Availability is confirmed directly by Palak.

How can I know the package price?

Package and service pricing depends on the makeup type and event requirements. Message Palak on WhatsApp for current details.

Is my booking confirmed after submitting the form?

No. The form sends an enquiry only. Your appointment is confirmed after Palak checks availability and agrees the details with you.
